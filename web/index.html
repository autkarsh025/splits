    How it works

    Equivalent times. Every time is converted to VDOT with the Daniels and Gilbert equations: the oxygen cost of running at your pace divided by the share of VO2max you can hold for that long. Inverting the same equations gives equivalent times at other distances and the training paces. Rankings against the marathon field use your time’s marathon equivalent unless you entered a marathon.

    Rankings. Percentiles are read from the full distribution of finish times, not from averages. College rankings use each athlete’s best time in that event.

    Limits. Marathon fields are self-selected, so beating 60% of them is better than beating 60% of all people. Equivalents assume you have trained for the distance. Course, weather and pacing move real results by minutes.
